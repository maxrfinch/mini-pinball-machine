# Cabinet Build Guide
![Mini Pinball Cabinet In Progress](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxrfinch/mini-pinball-machine/master/documentation/images/IMG_2406.JPG)

This document provides the high‑level process for assembling the acrylic cabinet for the Mini Pinball Machine. It is *not* exhaustive and should be used alongside professional acrylic fabrication tutorials. YouTube walkthroughs from experienced acrylic fabricators are highly recommended.

## Overview
This build assumes you ordered laser‑cut acrylic panels from SendCutSend. If you 3D‑printed your panels instead, the welding steps do not apply.

You will receive multiple flat panels that must be chemically welded together using Weld‑On 3 or Weld‑On 4. Weld‑On 3 is thinner and evaporates faster, while Weld‑On 4 provides more working time. Both work using capillary action.

## Required Supplies
- Weld‑On 3 **or** Weld‑On 4
- Applicator bottle with needle tip
- Large poster board or foam presentation board (base work surface)
- Alignment blocks (Jenga blocks work very well)
- Strong double‑sided tape (VHB or equivalent)
- M5 screws for initial alignment
- Safety gloves and ventilation

## Preparing the Work Surface
1. Lay the poster board flat.
2. Apply VHB tape to your alignment blocks.
3. Position blocks firmly so the acrylic panel cannot shift during welding.
4. Press everything down so securely that the acrylic and blocks will not move even if the board is lifted.

### When you're done it should look like this:
![Preparing first wall weld](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxrfinch/mini-pinball-machine/master/documentation/images/IMG_2400.JPG)

## Step 1: Identify Left vs. Right Panels
It is easy to accidentally make two left panels or two right panels. Ensure:
- You know which is your **left** wall and which is your **right** wall.
- Screw‑hole orientation matches the 3D model.
- The internal gutter channels face inward toward each other.

## Step 2: Assemble Outer and Inner Walls
Each side consists of:
- **Outer wall** (large piece with screw holes)
- **Inner wall** (interior structural wall)
- **Outer ring/bezel**

### Alignment Using M5 Screws
Insert M5 screws through the alignment holes in the outer wall and inner wall. This keeps these two components perfectly aligned.

### Positioning
1. Lay your chosen side (left or right) flat on the board against the Jenga alignment blocks.
2. Place the inner wall using M5 screws to align.
3. Place the outer ring/bezel on top.

Everything should now be dry‑fit and ready for adhesive.
![Outer, Inner, and bezel wall lined up with jenga blocks and screws](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maxrfinch/mini-pinball-machine/master/documentation/images/IMG_2403.JPG)

## Step 3: Welding the First Panel
Weld‑On behaves like a solvent — it *melts* the acrylic surfaces and fuses them.

### Important Safety Notes
- The liquid is extremely thin and toxic.
- Do **not** bend the needle; flicking will throw solvent and leave permanent streaks.
- Work slowly enough for capillary action to pull the solvent through the seam.

### Tack Weld
1. Apply tiny drops at a few corner points.
2. Let these set for ~60 seconds to hold the shape.
3. Confirm nothing shifted.

### Full Seam Weld
Once tacked:
- Run the needle slowly along the seams.
- Avoid over‑applying; Weld‑On wicks aggressively.
- Inspect for bubbles; Weld‑On 3 produces fewer bubbles than 4.

Let the panel cure for a few minutes, then remove the Jenga blocks and proceed to the next panel.

## Step 4: Build the Second Side
Repeat the same process for the opposite wall:
- Correct orientation
- Gutter channels facing inward
- M5 screw alignment
- Jenga‑block frame
- Tack weld → full weld

## Step 5: Prepare All Other Panels
Peel the protective backing from:
- Roof panel
- Back panel
- Floor panel
- Front button panel
- Two screen‑depth panels (stacked for 6 mm depth)
- Camera panel
- Matrix LED panel

Dry‑fit all of them to verify tolerance before welding.

## Step 6: Attach Structural Interior Panels
These panels **will be permanently welded**:
- Roof
- Back wall
- Floor
- Front button panel

### Serviceable Panels (Do NOT Weld)
- Screen panel (6 mm stack)
- Camera panel
- Matrix LED panel

These must remain removable to service wiring and hardware.

### Welding Order
1. Dry‑fit all structural panels inside one main side panel.
2. Tack‑weld small points.
3. Reinstall the opposite wall temporarily to ensure alignment.
4. Remove serviceable panels.
5. Complete all permanent seam welds.

## Step 7: Screen Panel Assembly
The screen requires two stacked acrylic frames for thickness.
- Align them using Jenga blocks on the poster board.
- Weld the perimeter.
- Allow to fully cure.

## Step 8: 3D‑Printed Standoffs and Inserts
Use heat‑set M5 threaded inserts:
1. Heat a soldering iron.
2. Press‑fit the insert into the standoff hole.
3. Let cool before handling.
4. Install standoffs into the main structural panel.

If holes are slightly oversized, a small amount of gel CA glue can help secure the insert.

## Step 9: Final Assembly
You should now be able to:
1. Screw one structural wall into the standoffs.
2. Place all interior panels.
3. Attach the opposite wall.
4. Verify everything fits snugly with no bowing or twisting.

This completes the cabinet shell. You are now ready to plan wire routing and install hardware.
